# WaveShelf 🌊📖

# 📖 Description

**WaveShelf** isn't just another boring spreadsheet for your books—it’s a high-vibe digital sanctuary for your literary soul! 🌊

Let’s face it: we’ve all finished a chapter and thought, *"I need to remember that quote forever,"* only to forget it ten minutes later. Or we’ve abandoned a book for a week and totally lost our place. WaveShelf was built to capture all your emotions and updates in one set!


### It’s a place where you can:

* **Watch your progress grow** with live bars that make hitting 100% feel like winning a marathon. 🏃‍♂️
* **Capture the feels** by tagging quotes with their emotional "vibe"—whether a passage made you cry, laugh, or want to conquer the world. 🎭
* **Be the critic** you were born to be with a dedicated space for reviews and ratings once the final page is turned.

> I built this app because books aren't just paper and ink, they're experiences. WaveShelf is here to make sure none of those experiences get lost in the sea of a busy life! 🌊📖

## ✨ Functionality 
* **Progress Tracking:** Real-time progress bars that update as you log your current page.
* **Status Automation:** Books automatically move from "To Read" to "Reading" or "Finished" based on your activity.
* **Quote Journal:** Save specific quotes that you like with page numbers and tag them with emotions (e.g., Inspired, Thrilled, Calm).
* **Book Reviews:** Write full reviews and provide star ratings once a book is completed.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used 
* **Language:** JavaScript (ES6+)
* **Runtime:** Node.js
* **Framework:** Express.js
* **Database:** MongoDB with Mongoose ODM
* **Templating:** EJS (Embedded JavaScript)
